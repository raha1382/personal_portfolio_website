    Where I've worked

    Experience

    Research Collaboration, NORC Lab

    Amirkabir University of Technology  |  Apr 2026 – Sep 2026

    Supervisor: Dr. Mehdi Ghatee

    • Identified a key limitation in federated graph learning arising from heterogeneity in feature domains and missing cross-subgraph neighbors for each client. These factors result in local bias that cannot be handled by standard federated learning.
    • Built FedHyPr-MNG, a hybrid framework that improves the personalization–generalization trade-off in federated graph learning by combining global node generation, cross-client prototype sharing, and a dual-encoder design. On our benchmarks, it outperformed baseline combinations of these components.
    • This project started as my bachelor's thesis, which was completed in Sep 2026, and more advanced versions are still under development with my supervisor.

    Summer Internship, PURE Program

    Sabanci University  |  Jul 2026 – Aug 2026

    Supervisor: Dr. Erhun Kundakcioglu

    • Worked on a learning-augmented optimization project for a time-dependent vehicle routing problem in last-mile delivery, where customers are grouped into flexible clusters and served within time windows.
    • Formulated a MILP model with cluster-level and multi-commodity constraints, solved it with Gurobi, and generated synthetic urban delivery datasets.
    • Compared the MILP against a DFS-Greedy baseline that assigns clusters to vehicles via depth-first search and then builds routes with greedy nearest-neighbor and 2-opt local search, cutting costs by 7.6–15.5%. The internship ended in Aug 2026, and the work continues with my supervisor, with more advanced versions under development and learning-based extensions planned.
